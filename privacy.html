Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

This service ("the service," "Claudette") is a personal, non-commercial SMS text messaging tool operated by an individual for use within a private group text conversation.

Information We Collect

The service processes phone numbers and message content sent to it in order to generate and send automated text replies within the group conversation.

How Information Is Used

Information is used solely to operate this messaging program: to receive messages, generate automated replies, and send those replies back within the same conversation. Ordinary conversational message content is processed by a third-party AI service provider strictly as a technical vendor assisting in delivering this messaging service (in the same way a phone carrier or platform provider is necessary to deliver a text message) — never for that vendor's own marketing, advertising, or promotional purposes, and never sold. Opt-in, opt-out, and consent keyword replies (YES, START, STOP, HELP) are handled directly by the messaging platform and are not forwarded to the AI service provider.

No Sharing for Marketing Purposes

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are excluded from all processing described above and will not be shared with any third parties for any purpose.

Message Frequency & Rates

Message frequency varies based on conversation activity. Message and data rates may apply.

Data Retention

Message data is retained only as long as needed to operate the service and may be deleted at any time upon request.

Opt-Out

Reply STOP at any time to a message from this service to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance.
