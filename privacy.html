Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

This service ("the service") is a personal, non-commercial SMS text messaging tool operated by an individual for use within a private group text conversation.

Information We Collect

The service processes phone numbers and message content sent to it in order to generate and send automated text replies. No information is sold, shared with third parties for marketing, or used for any purpose beyond operating this personal messaging tool.

How Information Is Used

Message content may be sent to a third-party AI service provider (such as Anthropic) solely to generate a reply. Phone numbers are used only to send and receive messages via Twilio.

Data Retention

Message data is retained only as long as needed to operate the service and may be deleted at any time upon request.

Opt-Out

Reply STOP at any time to a message from this service to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance.
